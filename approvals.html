Government & civil approvals we coordinate

A general reference for the authorities most fit-out projects touch in Dubai. This does not constitute legal or regulatory advice — always confirm current requirements directly with each authority.

Dubai Municipality

  • ScopeBuilding permits, structural changes
  • Typically required forVillas, apartments, offices

Dubai Civil Defence

  • ScopeFire & life safety compliance
  • Typically required forOffices, F&B, retail

DEWA

  • ScopeElectrical & water connections
  • Typically required forAll fit-out categories

Landlord / Building Management

  • ScopeNOC for interior alterations
  • Typically required forApartments, leased offices, retail units
