Common Questions

Commercial fit-out, answered

How fast can you fit out a retail or restaurant unit before opening day?

It depends on shell condition and scope, but fast-track fit-outs typically run design through construction in a matter of weeks once approvals are filed. We give a firm date after seeing the shell and your opening deadline — not before.

Do you handle Food Control and Civil Defence approvals for F&B units?

Yes — authority and landlord approvals are part of the standard turnkey scope for F&B and retail fit-outs, including Food Control, Civil Defence and DEWA where applicable.

Can you work within our landlord's NOC and mall design guidelines?

Yes — we coordinate directly with landlord/mall management on NOC requirements and design guideline compliance as part of the approvals process.

Do you fit out pre-leased shell & core office space?

Yes — shell & core to move-in-ready office fit-out is one of our core commercial services, coordinated with your building's rules and timeline.
