Trends We're Tracking

What's shaping fit-out right now

01

Biophilic Design

Natural materials, daylight-first layouts and planting integrated into both villas and offices, not just retail lobbies.

02

Smart & Connected Spaces

Lighting, climate and access control increasingly specified at the MEP stage, not bolted on after handover.

03

Hybrid Workspaces

Offices designed around a mix of focus rooms, collaboration zones and fewer fixed desks per head.

04

Sustainable Luxury

Premium clients asking about material sourcing and embodied carbon alongside finish quality.
