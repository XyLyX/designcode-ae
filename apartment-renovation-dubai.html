Common Questions

Apartment renovation, answered

How long does a 1BHK or 2BHK renovation take?

It depends on scope and how much layout change is involved, but apartment renovations are generally faster than full villa renovations. We give a firm, unit-specific timeline after the site assessment — not before.

Do I need building or landlord approval to renovate an apartment in Dubai?

In most buildings, yes — a landlord/building-management NOC is required before renovation work can start, and some works may need DEWA or Civil Defence sign-off. We handle this as part of the turnkey scope.

Can I stay in the apartment during renovation?

For lighter renovations, sometimes. For full-unit renovations involving flooring, MEP and layout changes throughout, most residents relocate for the construction phase. We'll advise once we've seen the scope.

What's the difference in scope between a Studio and a 3BHK renovation?

See the unit-type breakdown above — scope scales with bedroom count, from a Studio's open-plan kitchenette-and-bathroom scope up to a 3BHK's full-unit renovation with additional bedrooms, storage and MEP coordination.
