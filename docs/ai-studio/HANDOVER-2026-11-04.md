## Decision
Integrate an AI-powered design assessment and concept experience into the existing Design Code Studios website, NOT a standalone brand. Preserve turnkey design, engineering, procurement, and execution positioning. Do not deploy or implement until audit gaps are resolved and explicit approval is given.

## Source concept
Seven user-shared consumer AI interior prompts: (1) ranked room audit, (2) style makeover with before/after, (3) budget makeover and shopping list, (4) three furniture layouts, (5) colour/lighting, (6) renter-friendly redesign, (7) 48-hour implementation plan. These are useful ideation prompts, not validated architectural, electrical, dimensional, procurement or construction deliverables.

## Product strategy
Proposed route: /ai-design-studio (ai-design-studio.html in existing static site).
Customer modes:
- Analyse My Space: upload photographs, obtain three prioritised issues, broad indicative costs.
- Redesign My Space: style/budget preferences, AI concepts, layouts and renter-friendly options.
- Execute My Design: preliminary itemisation and execution outline; professional review, consultation and quotation.
Start with living rooms and bedrooms, one complete end-to-end workflow, not seven incomplete tools. Initial free limited audit/preview; paid detailed concept or consultation only after validating conversion and unit economics. Never present AI-generated renderings as measured drawings or indicative estimates as binding quotes.

## Proposed user journey
Upload photographs and dimensions -> select room type, budget, style and restrictions -> structured room audit -> one initial visual concept and revisions within limits -> indicative budget with assumptions -> contact capture, professional review and project quotation. Lead conversion, not image count, is the key outcome.

- Geometry and dimensions must not be claimed as reliable from photos alone.

- Mobile UX, failed-job retries, error states, non-lossy form submissions, reliable notification delivery, accessibility and GA4/GTM conversion QA.
- Do not automatically publish customer images or send them to AI services without disclosed consent.
