Why Design Code

The risk in most fit-out projects isn't design. It's coordination.

Splitting design, procurement and construction across separate firms is where budgets and timelines usually break. We don't split it.

01

One Team

No coordination between multiple contractors, no finger-pointing when something's late — your designer and your builder are the same people.

02

One Responsibility

A single point of accountability from design through handover. One contract, one team to answer to.

03

Built, Not Just Designed

Design decisions are developed with execution in mind, so what gets drawn is what actually gets built — on budget.

04

Controlled Delivery

Procurement, MEP, construction and finishing are managed together, not handed off between disconnected vendors.
