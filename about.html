Built as a single-source studio, on purpose

Design Code was founded in Dubai in 2019 on one idea: that fit-out fails when responsibility is split across too many hands. We keep design, procurement and site execution under one roof.

Company History

How the studio grew

2019

Founded in Dubai

Design Code opens as a residential fit-out practice, working across villas and furnished apartments in Dubai.

2021

Commercial Division Established

In-house MEP and project management capability added, extending the practice into office, retail and F&B fit-out.

2023

In-House Joinery & Procurement

A dedicated procurement and custom joinery function is brought in-house to shorten lead times and tighten cost control.

Today

500+ Projects, Two Sectors, One Standard

Design Code now runs parallel residential and commercial practices from a single Dubai studio.
