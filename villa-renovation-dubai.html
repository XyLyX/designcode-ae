Common Questions

Villa renovation, answered

How long does a full villa renovation take in Dubai?

It depends on scope and villa size, but most full interior renovations run from initial design through handover across several months once approvals are secured. We give a firm, project-specific timeline after the site assessment and design stage — not before.

Do I need Dubai Municipality approval for an interior renovation?

Most structural changes, MEP alterations and certain fit-out works require authority approval (Dubai Municipality, DEWA, and Civil Defence depending on scope). We handle the permitting process as part of the turnkey scope, so you're not managing separate applications yourself.

Can I stay in the villa during renovation?

For partial renovations, often yes — we can sequence work to keep parts of the home livable. For full gut renovations, most clients relocate for the construction phase. We'll advise on the right approach once we've seen the scope.

What's included in a turnkey villa renovation quote?

Concept design, authority permitting, fit-out construction, and landscaping and handover are all part of the standard turnkey scope — see the checklist above. Material sourcing, MEP engineering and project management are included; we'll confirm exact inclusions against your brief.
